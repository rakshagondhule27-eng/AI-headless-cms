Research Notebook

How the implementation can be explained, evaluated and extended as a final-year research project.

01 · PROBLEM

Why intelligent content management?

Traditional content workflows can require repetitive writing, tagging, categorization and review. The project investigates how local NLP and machine learning can assist these steps without removing editorial control.

03 · MEASUREMENT

What we measure

  • Accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score
  • Confusion matrix and class-level report
  • Content quality and readability indicators
  • API correctness and response behavior
  • Workflow and authorization behavior
04 · LIMITATIONS

Responsible interpretation

The bundled ML dataset is intentionally small and demonstrative. Real research should use a larger domain-specific dataset, documented sampling, reproducible splits and stronger baselines.
